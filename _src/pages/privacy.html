Privacy
How We Handle Your Details

Who we are. Rising Light UK. You can reach us at svn-77mediaofficial@outlook.com.

What we collect. What you send through our two forms: your name, your email address, your message or how you'd like to help, and the date you sent it. If you book The Clothes Run, also what you send us by Instagram message (see below).

Why. To reply to you and, if you'd like to volunteer or partner with us, to arrange it. We use your details because you've asked us to get back to you. If you choose to tell us something personal, such as about your health, we only use it to help you and handle it with extra care.

Who else sees it. We don't sell your details or give them to anyone else for their own use, and we don't add you to a mailing list. We would only pass them on if the law requires it, or if we believe someone is at serious risk of harm.

Where it's kept. In a database run for us by Supabase, our database provider (its servers are in Ireland), and in our email inbox (Microsoft Outlook) when we reply, or if the form sends by email instead. Only Rising Light's founder has access to them. Clothes Run bookings are kept in our Instagram messages.

How long. We keep enquiries for up to 24 months, then delete them, including any emails about them.

The Clothes Run. If you book a collection, you send us your postcode by Instagram message, and your address once we've agreed a day. We use them only to arrange and make the collection. Only our founder can see them. Instagram is run by Meta, so Meta holds the messages too. We delete your address and our messages about the collection within 7 days of collecting.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or ask us to stop using them, by emailing us. We'll reply within a month. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

The rest of the site. No tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser remembers your light or dark choice on your own device. The site is hosted by GitHub, our events and forms run on Supabase, our fonts load from Google Fonts and the event viewer from Cloudflare's code library, so those companies see your IP address, as with any site that uses them.
