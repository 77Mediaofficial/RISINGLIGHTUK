CH. 08 — Questions
Questions

What we do, where we work, and how you can help.

What is Rising Light UK?
Rising Light UK is a grassroots humanitarian initiative working under one belief — that compassion has no borders. We help people and animals in need across different countries, running hands-on projects and sharing the stories behind them.
Where do you work?
We're currently active in the United Kingdom (London and Surrey), and we grow into new places as projects take shape. In 2026 we also worked in Guatemala (February to April) and spent one week in April in the United States (Arizona), handing out water on the streets. Tap a location on the map in Our Work to explore our work in each place.
How can I get involved?
Volunteer your time, partner with us, lend a skill (translation, media, logistics, trades), or simply help spread the word. Head to Get Involved and send us a message — we'd love to hear from you.
Do I need to give money to help?
Not at all. Some of the most valuable support is time, skills and reach — volunteering, partnerships, and sharing our work with people who might be able to help. Every bit of momentum counts.
Are you a registered charity?
No, not at the moment. Rising Light UK is a volunteer initiative, not a registered charity. If that changes, we will show our charity number here.
How do I contact you?
Email us at svn-77mediaofficial@outlook.com, use the Get Involved form, or follow @risinglightuk on Instagram.
