Safeguarding
Keeping People Safe

Rising Light UK is a small volunteer group. At our events, on our projects and through Quiet Battles, we meet people who may be going through a hard time. Keeping them safe matters more than anything else we do.

How we work. Our volunteers listen, sit with people and help them reach trained support. They are not counsellors, doctors or crisis workers, and we do not replace professional help.

What we keep private. We keep what people tell us private. We would only pass it on if the law requires it, or if we believe someone is at serious risk of harm.

If we're worried. If we are worried about a child or an adult at risk, we tell the safeguarding team at the local council. If a crime may have happened, we tell the police. When we work outside the UK, we keep to the same standards and follow the law of that country.

Children and young people. We do not run activities for under-18s unless a parent or carer is there.

Who to contact. Our safeguarding lead is our founder, Joe Chapman. Email svn-77mediaofficial@outlook.com with "Safeguarding" in the subject line. We will reply within 2 working days.

If someone is in danger now. Call 999, or go straight to A&E (Accident and Emergency).

If you need to talk to someone now. Free crisis lines are listed on our Quiet Battles page.
