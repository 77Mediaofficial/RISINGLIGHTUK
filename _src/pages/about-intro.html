Who We Are

Rising Light UK is a grassroots humanitarian initiative working under one belief — that compassion has no borders.

We help people and animals in need across different countries, running hands-on projects and sharing the stories behind them. We're currently active in the United Kingdom (London) and Guatemala, and we grow into new places as projects take shape. In 2026 we also spent one week in the United States (Arizona), handing out water on the streets.

Rising Light UK was founded in London by Joe Chapman, who runs it with a small team of volunteers. It is not registered with the Charity Commission or Companies House, and so far it is self-funded.
