N°01

The Clothes Run

We collect the clothes and shoes you no longer wear and take them to a charity clothing bank.

Where
London and Surrey
When
Starting now
Cost
Free. Nothing is kept or sold.

How It Works

  1. Book

    Message @risinglightuk (opens Instagram in a new tab) your postcode on Instagram, as a direct message, not in the comments.

  2. Collect

    We reply to agree a day and tell you who’s coming, then collect from your doorstep. We only ask for your full address once a day is agreed.

  3. Drop off

    We take everything to a charity clothing bank.

What the Banks Ask For

  • Clean and dry
  • Shoes in pairs
  • Carrier bags, not bin bags
  • No duvets or pillows

Every clothing bank has its own list.

Source: council guidance from Islington, Reigate and Banstead, and Mole Valley.

Why

118

The average UK adult has 118 items of clothing, and a quarter haven’t been worn for at least a year.

Source: WRAP (Waste and Resources Action Programme), 2022.
