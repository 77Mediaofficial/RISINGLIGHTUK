Accessibility
Using This Website

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This page says what works, what doesn't yet, and how to tell us about a problem.

What works. Every page starts with a "Skip to content" link, the first thing the Tab key reaches. Every link, button and form field we found can be reached and used with the keyboard, in reading order, and the focused item gets a 2px high-contrast outline. The light/dark button works with Enter or Space and says whether dark mode is on. Both forms on the Get Involved page can be filled in with the keyboard alone, every field has a visible label, and there is no CAPTCHA. Pages have a header, labelled navigation, a main area and a footer; images have text descriptions; every page has its own title and declares English as its language. In both themes, all the text we measured meets the AA contrast level. Pages reflow to a 320px-wide screen (the same as 400% zoom) without sideways scrolling, and you can pinch-zoom. If your device asks for reduced motion, the globe, the map lines and the progress-bar shimmer stay still and smooth scrolling is off. There are no auto-playing slideshows, no video or audio, and no time limits.

The disaster-alerts globe. The spinning globe on Our Work is mouse and touch only and is hidden from screen readers, but its alerts are a text list that works fully with the keyboard, and alert levels are written in words. The globe turns until you interact with it; focusing the list pauses it, and reduced motion stops it.

Known problems. When you move backwards with Shift+Tab, the sticky header can cover the focused item; on Quiet Battles it can fully hide the CALM phone number link. On the Quiet Battles and Privacy pages the section titles are styled text, not real headings, so you cannot jump between sections by heading. Most links that open a new tab do not warn you first. The outlines of the form fields are faint, though each field has a clear label. Text sizes are fixed, so the browser's "text size only" setting has little effect: use browser zoom (Ctrl or Cmd and +) instead. In Windows high-contrast mode the forms' Send buttons look like plain text. The event document viewer shows PDF pages as images and does not move keyboard focus into itself; use its Download button, or email us for the content in another format. The site opens in a dark theme whatever your device setting is.

Without JavaScript. Most text, navigation and contact details work without it. The events list, live alerts, Instagram strip, About figures and the forms need it, so without JavaScript please email us instead.

How we tested. We tested the site ourselves on 28 October 2026 in Google Chrome at desktop and phone sizes, in both themes, with and without reduced motion, using keyboard checks, contrast measurements and the axe-core automated checker. We have not yet tested other browsers, real phones or screen readers, and this site has not had an independent audit.
