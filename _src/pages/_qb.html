CH. 06 — Quiet Battles
Quiet Battles Rising Light UK

Our mental health and suicide prevention work, running alongside everything else we do.

Some of the people we visit are fighting something nobody can see. Quiet Battles is the part of Rising Light UK that says so out loud — and makes sure that no one we meet has to work out where to turn on their own.

What we do

  • Listen, without flinching
  • Sit with you while you make the call
  • Point you to people who are trained

What we don't

  • Diagnose
  • Counsel
  • Replace a professional

We're volunteers, not clinicians. That's the promise, and it's the whole promise.

If today is hard

  • Samaritans 116 123 24/7 · Free from any phone · No credit needed
  • Shout Text 85258 24/7 · Text the word SHOUT · No call needed
  • CALM 0800 58 58 58 5pm to midnight, every day of the year
  • NHS urgent mental health 111 → option 2 24/7 · England
  • If life is in danger now 999 Or go straight to Accident & Emergency

You don't have to be in crisis to call any of these. Wanting to talk is enough.

Quiet Battles goes out on the same rounds as everything else we do. Come with us.
